# ModernPapers provenance

The reader and publishing format are derived from Kevin Bryan's ModernPapers:

- Repository: https://github.com/kevincure/ModernPapers
- Pinned commit: `0349ca2793a5d6ccc2111b48b824985221f07032`
- Commit date: 2026-07-09
- License: MIT

This implementation separates the authoring pipeline, static reader, and AI
proxy; removes public upload and API-key interfaces; adds draft deployment
gates; and validates extraction completeness before publication. Upstream
updates are reviewed and merged manually rather than pulled automatically.
